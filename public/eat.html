Maze muncher 🟡

A tiny maze-chomp game, built in vanilla JS on an HTML5 canvas. Eat every dot, dodge the ghosts, and grab a power pellet to turn the tables.

Level 1

Controls

  • Arrow keys / WASD — move
  • Eat a pulsing power pellet to turn ghosts blue and vulnerable for a few seconds
  • Clear every dot to advance a level — ghosts get a little faster each time
